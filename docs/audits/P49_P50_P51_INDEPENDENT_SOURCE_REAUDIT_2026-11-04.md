**Concrete design scenario:** A single 120-question APFC paper has a *single timed exam section* with Labour, Accountancy and Economy questions. Today the authored `section` field must either remain “General” (all three subjects become **General** in the taxonomy) **or** be split into three separately navigable/timed sections merely to get correct subject roots. That is an architectural mismatch, not an aesthetic preference. Repair with distinct `sectionId/sectionTitle` for exam policy and `subjectId/subjectLabel` for semantic taxonomy; retain author-chosen topics and avoid a manufactured official subtopic corpus.

**Recommended bound:** do not create a large remote syllabus ontology or migrations. Add a minimal local hierarchical taxonomy domain with canonical subject roots (official APFC headings only when explicitly chosen; custom subject option elsewhere), reusable author/notebook pickers, stable per-question subject/topic/subtopic metadata, correct parent rollups including subject-only items, stable **underlying question identity** across editions without merging conflicting answers, and readable parent-aware builder labels. New tests must prove mixed-subject *single* exam section, absent subtopic, repeated attempts, source/reviewed revisions and cross-exam isolation.
