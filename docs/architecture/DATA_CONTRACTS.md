## Immutable content

- Exam identity; immutable paper/answer-key/question revision IDs; editorial statuses; data/media/stimulus IDs and hashes. Questions belong to exactly one paper section, answer-key entries refer to the precise frozen revision, and the content hashes prevent silent mutation.
- Six kinds: `single-choice`, `multiple-select`, `numeric-entry`, `true-false`, `matching`, `free-response`. Rich passages/cases, tables, chart/image assets are packaged as self-contained records. `source`, `reviewed`, `custom` are distinct editions.
- Validated `ExamPackage` is imported as JSON, not executable code. Source CGL JSON, review patches, and solutions have identical Git blob SHAs to baseline/v11. Corrections are not official exam answer keys.

## Session and assessment

- `SessionState` tracks elapsed timer time, current section elapsed, foreground question exposure, responses, genuine visits, completed sections, visibility and clock origin. **Pass 18** adds optional `completedSectionTimings` to NEW sessions, capturing each closed sequential/single section's consumed timer milliseconds and closure reason. Older saved attempts lack this property; unrestricted multi-section papers cannot meaningfully attribute wall time to individual sections. Never reconstruct old section timers from foreground exposure. `SessionEvent` is the transition interface; immutable frozen attempt persists selected mode, question revision, score policy, resources and answers.
- Rules support untimed, whole-paper and sectional budgets; pause policy; free/forward-only question navigation; free/sequential section navigation; positive, negative and unanswered marks; overrides per section, kind and key item; partial credit and separate written/manual grades. **Pass 17 executable semantics:** untimed/whole-paper sequential-locked sections advance only by explicit `SUBMIT_SECTION`; whole-paper time stays continuous; sectional timing requires sequential-locked sections. Automatic sectional expiry advances immediately, whereas manual expiry enters a frozen `section-expired` state and requires `SUBMIT_SECTION` without accruing waiting time or allowing answer changes. Untimed sectional sections may be advanced explicitly even when early submission is otherwise forbidden. `allowReview=false` withholds the post-completion detailed question/key/solution review UI but **does not** prevent in-exam navigation or suppress numerical results and time analytics. It is a UI disclosure policy, not cryptographic protection of embedded offline content.
- **CGL editorial user decision:** source-invalid is excluded in source edition; unpatched invalid/caution converts to scoreable reviewed caution retaining publisher key. The dispute of 6 October 2026 Q62 is *visible after submission*. No independent correction is claimed.
- **No premature disclosure** of keys, worked explanations, edited answer comparison, editorial dispute notes or marked solution until complete.
- **Validated score envelope (Pass 17):** `incorrectMarks ≤ unansweredMarks ≤ correctMarks`, `correctMarks ≥ 0`, `incorrectMarks ≤ 0`, and bounded finite marking values (absolute magnitude ≤ 1,000,000). Component-based partial credit cannot exceed `correctMarks`. `scoreableMaximum` is the nominal full-credit ceiling; `scoreableLowerBound` is a **conservative bound, not necessarily an attainable floor**, accounting for potentially negative component penalties. Pending written responses stay outside the automated ceiling. These rules apply at package/frozen-record validation; historic unvalidated source should never be silently rescored or rewritten.

## Persistence

- Store types: `generic-attempt`, `generic-package`, `learning-card`. Stored attempts recalculate frozen item verdicts/totals on read; answer event continuity, ids, hashes, provenance and manual grades are validated.
- IndexedDB with per-record `saveRevision` compare-and-swap; fallback localStorage has cross-tab stamp conflicts; emergency mirror and explicitly labelled unsaved recovery export. Browser data remains subject to eviction. **Pass 18 deletion:** ordinary `Delete` applies to completed attempts only; `Discard unfinished` has two confirmations, status/type and version rechecks inside the transaction, and matching emergency-mirror cleanup. Notebook cards use a distinct deletion intent.
- **Current backup v4:** `practice-desk-backup` compact attempt rows + unique content-hashed snapshots, appearance/optional family setups, SHA-256 manifest and count/export time; verify both envelope and referenced snapshots before additive no-overwrite merge. Earlier v3 readers are not retained, and no schema migration is required.
- `LearningCard` saves exact question/key/media and manually confirmed error label, note, bookmark and FSRS review logs; mistake revision generates a new untimed custom paper, never substitutes ratings for exam marks.
- Historical cases have absent visit counters or section timer histories when unknown. Stored optional section-time evidence is checked against frozen section identity/order, timer budgets, elapsed totals and closure. Results distinguish consumed timer time, foreground question exposure, attempted/non-excluded response rate, and the global median of measured scored-answer exposure. Pause/manual-expiry waiting does not accrue timer time. Trend comparisons require the same frozen revision, key and mode; do not infer national rank or percentile.

## Historical scoring and comparability (Pass 19)

- Completed archive entries and latest finished-result cards now show **frozen automated earned / eligible maximum marks**. The denominator comes from the attempt's frozen paper/key, excludes editorially unscored and separately manually graded written items, and is never fabricated for an unfinished attempt.
- The **cross-paper marks-priority view** is a separate descriptive diagnostic, not the exact-paper longitudinal trend. It selects up to eight most recent compatible full-paper attempts and requires two different source papers. Match exam, edition, mode, section order/count/kind, per-item effective grading rules, section clock budgets, navigation and pause policies. Section timer identities are paper-specific, so compare ordered budgets, not raw section IDs. Rank each section by the **sum of frozen earned marks / sum of that section's actual eligible maximum**, lowest first; show negative values honestly; leave zero-max sections out of the marks ratio. The existing accuracy analysis remains separate.
- Cross-paper scores are **not difficulty-adjusted**, do not predict a rank/percentile and must not be pooled across unrelated exams. Exact-paper/key/mode remains mandatory for longitudinal trend claims.
- Multiple unfinished tests remain independent resumable records with a preview of five and a status-filtered View all link; three latest completed-result cards are never displaced by pending attempts.

## Pre-test scoring and educational provenance (Pass 20)

- The library preflight, complete rules panel and actual Start action use the **same configured executable package** (built-in strict/flexible/single CGL policy or imported exam policy), not a generic source edition or CGL hardcoded mark count. `scoreableMaximum` calculates exact automated eligible marks; editor-excluded and separately manual/free-response questions are disclosed by aggregate counts, not silently granted score credit. Key, section and kind overrides determine correct/wrong/blank rule ranges; partial-credit status is identified without disclosing answer options.
- Built-in CGL source and reviewed editions are distinguished by trusted bundled ID (not an unverified examId string) and may show source caution/exclusion, reviewed correction/caution status, and **scoped** patch counts. Individual corrections, disputed keys, question answer labels and solutions are never revealed until permitted after submission. Reviewed labels on externally imported exams do *not* certify independent accuracy or official affiliation; provenance for untrusted imports remains author supplied.
- The full rule/help panel names selected clock budgets, early-submission policy, unused sectional time, manual/automatic expiry, pausing, locking/backtracking, post-test review policy and actual keyboard shortcuts. It explains offline browser-local storage, export/checksum backups, and non-synchronized account limitations.
- The persistent header Display control stays accessible while scrolled, including a compact immersive-mode strip; its popover remains within the viewport at tested 195px zoom-equivalent width. The global footer states independent educational use and generic non-affiliation, with SSC-specific claims only in built-in CGL preflight. Physical-device/assistive-tech signoff remains separate.

## Contextual media, editorial review, pause and keyboard (Pass 21)

- Bundled original/reviewed CGL question and option media now carries immutable question-number/option-letter alt labels. Imported asset `altText` takes priority over contextual fallback (question figure, option, passage, matching item or chart); the fallback does not pretend to describe semantic diagram details.
- Post-test editorial status labels are descriptive, **not grading outcomes**. `verified` means *marked verified by author/source*, not officially authenticated; a `corrected` label becomes `Key corrected` only when the frozen editorial change metadata explicitly includes `answer-key`. `caution` never establishes the disputed key; `invalid/draft/under-review/incomplete-source/superseded` indicate excluded automatic scoring. Remain hidden during the active exam.
- In a deliberately paused timed exam, only **the current question's flag annotation** can be toggled when `allowFlagging=true`; answer, clear, navigation, submit, timing and visit counts remain frozen. Manual-expired and completed sessions reject flag mutation. UI disables paused navigation while preserving Flag/M.
- With focus on a native answer radio, unmodified ArrowLeft/ArrowRight select the radio choice; use **Alt+left/right** for question navigation. Without focused radios, unmodified arrows navigate within paper rules. A–D still chooses single-choice responses. Text entry, selects, composition, pause (other than M) and open Display settings respect native key/focus rules.
- The Pass 21 browser and source tests are targeted, not proof of physical screen-reader speech or mobile assistive-technology parity. Preserve this contract while doing Pass 22 input-draft and results DOM work.

## Incomplete multi-select drafts, matching conflict feedback and lazy results (Pass 22)

- Multi-select answers with a declared minimum/maximum are **scored only on valid selected counts**; `0` selections CLEAR. A nonzero invalid partial/excess selection is represented by a visible, explicitly **unscored and unsaved** draft retained only in the current browser tab. Any prior frozen saved response to that question is cleared through the normal session engine when the draft becomes invalid, so the old answer cannot secretly count. Drafts may survive in-tab question navigation but are not included in backups or surviving reload. When valid, current selections commit using the engine's standard ANSWER event. Users are not promised persistence of the draft.
- Matching right-hand options are unique across left-hand entries. Duplicate targets are identified on **every conflicting native select**, surfaced as `aria-invalid` with labelled `role=status` feedback; Save is blocked until duplicates are corrected using ordinary keyboard/mouse controls. A corrected selection is still uncommitted until Save, and the core response validator remains authoritative. No matching controls silently reassign a selected target.
- Result tabs retain the three structural `tabpanel` targets, `aria-controls` and keyboard focus, but **only the selected pane** generates complete DOM. Overview-only metrics/source-key, Review-only 100-item markup and frozen media/source comparisons, and Timing-only question ledger are computed on their own active pane. Inactive panels are empty `hidden` placeholders, not prefetched answers or solution text. When `allowReview=false`, detailed review remains withheld rather than materialized offscreen. Review filter, timing-to-review link, manual grades and notebook actions are preserved.
- The direct 100-item Chromium `file://` comparison against frozen Pass 21 used **4,337 DOM elements** across every tab before this change; Pass 22 uses **246** on Overview, **2,892** on Review and **1,231** on Timing. These are measurable reductions in DOM construction, **not** claims of uniform runtime speed; observed Overview tab-switch times were slightly slower on this CI machine.
